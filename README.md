## About Me

I build AI systems at the intersection of **machine learning, large language models, and software engineering**. My focus is on turning complex problems into practical, measurable solutions — from retrieval-augmented generation and multi-agent orchestration to predictive modeling and computer vision.

With a background in mathematics and over three years of professional experience in technical production, I bring analytical thinking, engineering discipline, and hands-on workflow optimization to AI development.

- **Generative AI:** LLM integration, semantic retrieval, reranking, and RAG evaluation.
- **Agentic AI:** Multi-agent architectures, tool orchestration, structured workflows, and safety guardrails.
- **Machine Learning:** Feature engineering, model tuning, explainability, and predictive analytics.
- **AI Engineering:** API development, containerization, local LLM inference, and model deployment.
